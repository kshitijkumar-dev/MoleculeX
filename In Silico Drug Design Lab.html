01 · Molecular docking

What docking is, and a demo

Docking predicts how a small molecule (ligand) might fit into a protein’s pocket and estimates a binding score. More negative ΔG usually means stronger predicted binding.

1 · Prepare

Get the protein structure and the ligand’s 3D shape.

2 · Search poses

Software (e.g. AutoDock Vina) tries many positions and rotations in the pocket.

3 · Score

A scoring function estimates binding energy for each pose.

4 · Validate

Top hits must be tested in the lab. A score is a prediction, not proof.
