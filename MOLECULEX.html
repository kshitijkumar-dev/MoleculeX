Why drug discovery is difficult

A medicine must work on its target, be safe, have suitable properties, be manufacturable and succeed in clinical testing. Many candidates fail along the way.

US$2.6 billion [2]

Average cost of a new drug, as cited by the report’s survey source. Not a guarantee for every drug.

More than 10 years [2]

Typical time cited by the same source.

Where AI helps

Prioritising candidates and spotting patterns so fewer weak candidates reach costly experiments. AI does not decide whether a medicine is safe or effective.

Case studies

Designing DDR1 inhibitors in 21 days [5]

GENTRL, a deep generative model, designed molecules in 21 days; six inhibitors were designed and four tested compounds were active in biochemical assays. Timeline in the dashboard above. A specific study, not a universal guarantee of speed.

Searching for new antibiotics [6]

A network trained on 2,335 molecules was applied to libraries of more than 107 million; 23 predictions were tested and eight were antibacterial. Halicin was found separately via the Drug Repurposing Hub. Funnel in the dashboard.

Predicting protein structures [7]

AlphaFold predicts 3D structures from amino acid sequences. CASP14 errors are charted above. It does not alone solve all protein biology.

    About the project

    MOLECULEX is an educational, literature-based website developed as an interactive companion to the Biology for Engineers experiential learning project. It presents published findings on AI in drug discovery, including screening, generative design, protein structure prediction and clinical development.

    Why MOLECULEX Is Not a Drug Discovery Tool

    MOLECULEX is an educational, literature-based project. It does not train or run real drug-discovery AI models, perform molecular docking, predict clinical outcomes or evaluate medicines. Its screening laboratory uses predefined fictional demonstration data. All scientific findings displayed in the research dashboard are drawn from the published sources listed in the references.
